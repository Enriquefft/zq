# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

zq is a technical project. Contributions and discussions should stay focused on improving the software. Be direct, be precise, and be respectful of others' time.

Expected behavior:

- Communicate clearly and technically
- Accept and give constructive criticism
- Respect that people have different experience levels and backgrounds
- Stay on topic in issues and pull requests

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Sustained off-topic disruption

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported to **Enriquefft** via [GitHub](https://github.com/Enriquefft). Reports will be handled privately and promptly. Consequences range from a warning to a permanent ban depending on severity.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other official communication channels.
